# News Outlet — Reader App

> Interactive app prototype for digital news outlets, featuring an editorial feed, immersive article reading and video content.

`HTML5` · `CSS3` · `JavaScript` · `Tailwind CSS (CDN)`

## 🇬🇧 English

**Segment:** Digital news & media

Interactive app prototype for digital news outlets, featuring an editorial feed, immersive article reading and video content.

**Highlights**

- Editorial feed with breaking-news tagging
- Immersive reader with sections, quotes and reading time
- Video hub and saved-articles area

**Tech:** HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS via CDN.

**Run it:** Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step, no dependencies to install.

_This is a design prototype: data is mocked in the front-end and there is no back-end._
